Modal

tier 2 — classes

The chrome only: .modal-backdrop dims and centres, .modal is the panel, and header / body / footer are three optional rows. You own the open-and-close state and the content.

Z-order: the backdrop sits at 500. Anything you layer above a modal must respect the shared chain — spotlight 510 < popover 550 < toast 600 < tips and the reconnect banner 1000. Do not invent a new z-index; pick the rung you belong on.

Destructive confirm

Name the consequence in the body, and make the confirming button the destructive one.

Scrolling body

Cap the body with max-height and overflow-y:auto, then add .dr-scroll to theme the scrollbar and add the fading scroll edges. .dr-scroll styles a scroll container; it does not create one. Inside a .modal the library already re-points --scroll-bg at the elevated surface, so the fade matches the panel instead of showing as a dark band.
